Our story

Skincare built on data

GlowGenic is a skincare technology company developing AI-powered skin analysis, personalised routines, and privacy-first progress tracking.

The problem

Most people are guessing at their own skin

Research published in the Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology shows that most people cannot accurately identify their own skin type. Yet the entire skincare industry asks you to self-diagnose before recommending anything.

GlowGenic gives everyday users access to skin insights that have traditionally been available mainly in professional beauty and dermatology settings, from their phone, on their schedule.

We pair clinical-grade skin imaging AI with large language models that turn raw data into a routine you can actually follow. Then we build the habit layer that keeps you consistent long enough to see results.

Our principles
Science first
Every recommendation is grounded in dermatological research and real skin data, not marketing copy or guesswork.
Honest progress
We track consistency alongside results. Your streak data sits alongside your Glow Score, showing the full picture, not just the flattering one.
Privacy by default
Your skin data and photos are yours. We never sell personal data to third parties. Full stop.
Consistency rewarded
Dermatology shows routines can take 4 to 8 weeks to show results. Our streak system is built to keep you consistent long enough for the science to work.
The technology

Two AI systems. One seamless experience.

Skin analysis
Computer vision ML
Professional-grade skin imaging technology analyses hydration, pore visibility, pigmentation, and clarity from a selfie. GlowGenic is built to integrate this data into a simple consumer experience.
Routine generation
Large language model
Your skin analysis data is processed by an AI routine engine that generates personalised steps, product categories, and timing, then updates as your skin data changes.
